## Dictato 0.1.0

The first release you can hand to someone else.

- Hebrew and English dictation, running entirely on your Mac. Audio never leaves it.
- A hotkey starts and stops recording; the text is pasted where you are typing.
- Preferences for models, profiles, hotkeys, microphones and word replacements.
- Dictato now updates itself. It checks once a day and always asks first.

This build is signed by its author rather than by Apple, so the first launch
needs one extra step. See INSTALL.md.
