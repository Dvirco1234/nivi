## Nivi 0.2.0

Mostly fixes. If Nivi has crashed or stopped listening on you, this is the update.

- Fixed a crash in the middle of dictating, in the modes that show words while you speak.
- Fixed a crash when clicking into a long transcript to copy part of it.
- Nivi no longer quits by itself when the Mac wakes from sleep.
- If the Mac's sound system gets stuck, only one dictation fails. Before, Nivi stopped
  recording until the sound system recovered, which once took 27 minutes.
- Starting a recording can no longer freeze the whole app.
- When "keep dictations out of clipboard history" is on, it now also works in Slack,
  VS Code, Arc and other apps built on web technology.
- Transcribed files can be saved as Text, Word, Rich Text or OpenDocument. Hebrew sits
  on the right in Word, as it should.
- Transcribed files are listed on the Transcribe a file tab, and stand out in History.
- The close, minimise and zoom buttons in Preferences respond across the whole button.

This build is signed by its author rather than by Apple, so the first launch
needs one extra step. See INSTALL.md.
